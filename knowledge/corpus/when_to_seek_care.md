---
title: When to Seek Care for Possible Heart Symptoms

Heart attack warning signs can include chest discomfort (pressure, squeezing,
fullness, or pain lasting more than a few minutes or that comes and goes),
discomfort in one or both arms, the back, neck, jaw, or stomach, shortness of
breath, breaking out in a cold sweat, nausea, or lightheadedness. Symptoms
can vary between individuals and are sometimes less typical in women, who
may experience shortness of breath, nausea, or back/jaw pain without the
classic chest pressure.

Any of these symptoms, especially if sudden, severe, or unexplained, warrant
immediate medical attention -- calling emergency services rather than
waiting to see if symptoms pass or trying to self-diagnose using general
information or online tools.

Educational tools that estimate cardiovascular risk from health data are
meant to support awareness and conversations with a healthcare provider, not
to replace emergency evaluation or a clinical diagnosis. Risk estimates,
however calculated, are not a substitute for professional medical assessment.
